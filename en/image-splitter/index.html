SPLIT IMAGES INTO GRIDS, PAGES AND POSTERS

Three ways to split one picture

An image splitter cuts one picture into several smaller images by grid, by height or by paper size. This tool previews every cut line and number before export, keeps full resolution and adds no watermark.

Grid split for Instagram and more

Any layout from 1×1 to 10×10, with presets for 3×3 and 2×2 grids and seamless carousels. Crop or pad tiles to 1:1, 4:5 or 3:4 automatically.

Long screenshots to pages

Split by A4 ratio, phone screen, pixel height or number of parts. Smart cuts look for a blank row near each cut and export images or an A4 PDF.

Poster printing on A4 or Letter

Choose A4, A3 or Letter and the number of pages across. The tool calculates pages, finished size and print dpi, then builds a PDF with cut marks, overlap and labels.

How to split an image online

  1. Choose, drop or paste the image you want to split.
  2. Pick Grid, Long image or Print poster, then choose a preset or set rows, columns, shape or paper.
  3. Check cut lines, numbers and the finished size in the preview. Click any tile to download just that one.
  4. Download every piece as a ZIP or PDF. On a phone, Share / Save to Photos saves all images at once.

Image splitter questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Opening, splitting, previewing, zipping and PDF creation all happen in this browser. Your first ZIP or PDF download loads open-source compression and PDF components; the image itself is never sent anywhere.

How do I split a picture into a 3×3 grid for Instagram?

Choose the 3×3 grid preset, set the tile shape to 3:4 or 4:5 to match the Instagram profile grid, check the kept area in the preview and download the ZIP. Set File numbering to Instagram posting order and post the files from post01 upward.

Why do Instagram grid posts go in reverse order?

Instagram shows the newest post at the top left of your profile, so a mosaic is posted from the bottom-right tile back to the top-left one. Post whole rows (multiples of 3) so the picture stays aligned.

Why do some tiles differ by one pixel?

When the width or height does not divide evenly by the rows or columns, the spare pixels are spread across the tiles, so neighbors differ by at most one pixel. Put back together, the tiles match the original exactly with no missing or repeated pixels.

How do I cut a long screenshot into A4 pages as a PDF?

Switch to Long image, choose A4 page ratio, keep Avoid cutting through text on, and select Download PDF. Each section fills one A4 page, and cuts move to the nearest blank row so lines of text are not sliced in half.

How do I print one image across several A4 pages?

Switch to Print poster, pick the paper and orientation, and set the pages across, for example 3. The tool works out the rows, finished size and dpi. Print the PDF at 100% (actual size), trim along the cut marks and glue the overlaps in label order: A1, A2 and so on.

Why does my printed poster look blurry?

Check the dpi in the result box. Aim for at least 150 dpi; below about 100 dpi the print looks soft up close. Use fewer pages across, smaller paper, or a higher-resolution source, because enlarging cannot add detail.

Is it free, and are there watermarks or limits?

It is free with no sign-up and no watermark. To keep the browser stable, one export holds up to 100 images or 64 poster pages, and the source image can be up to 50 MB or 64 megapixels.

Which formats work, and can I use it on a phone?

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, GIF (first frame) and other images your browser can open. Export as PNG, JPG, ZIP or PDF. It works in mobile browsers too, and phones with system sharing show Share / Save to Photos to save every tile at once.
