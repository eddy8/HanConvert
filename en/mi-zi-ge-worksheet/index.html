How to use it

  1. Enter the Chinese characters, words, or short sentence you want to practise.
  2. Open the preset and adjust the grid, tracing, Pinyin, stroke order, rows, and columns.
  3. Check the print preview, then print the sheet or save it as a PDF from your browser.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I save the mi zi ge worksheet generator as a PDF?

Yes. Generate the preview, choose Print, and select Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog.

Is my content uploaded?

No. After you open the main tool, supported extraction, glyph conversion, and worksheet layout are performed locally in your browser.

Why does this page open the main tool?

This page explains the task and supplies a focused preset. The shared tool keeps conversion behaviour consistent across every scenario.
