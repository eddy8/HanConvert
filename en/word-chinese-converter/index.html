How to use it

  1. Choose the matching file format and confirm that it contains selectable text.
  2. Open the file converter and select Simplified, Traditional, Taiwan, or Hong Kong Chinese.
  3. Review the extracted text before copying the result, and keep the original file for reference.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the Word Chinese converter preserve document formatting?

No. It extracts text for character-form conversion and copyable output; it does not rebuild a formatted Word document.

Is my content uploaded?

No. After you open the main tool, supported extraction, glyph conversion, and worksheet layout are performed locally in your browser.

Why does this page open the main tool?

This page explains the task and supplies a focused preset. The shared tool keeps conversion behaviour consistent across every scenario.
