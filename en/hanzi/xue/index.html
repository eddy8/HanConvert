Questions about the character 学

How is 学 pronounced in Mandarin?

学 is pronounced xué in Mandarin. It means to study or learn; knowledge, school, or a field of study.

What are the radical and stroke count for 学?

学 uses the 子 radical and has 8 strokes in the form shown on this page.

What are the Simplified and Traditional forms of 学?

The common Simplified form is 学; the Traditional form is 學.
