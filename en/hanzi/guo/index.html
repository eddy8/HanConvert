Questions about the character 国

How is 国 pronounced in Mandarin?

国 is pronounced guó in Mandarin. It means country, nation, state, or national territory.

What are the radical and stroke count for 国?

国 uses the 囗 radical and has 8 strokes in the form shown on this page.

What are the Simplified and Traditional forms of 国?

The common Simplified form is 国; the Traditional form is 國.
