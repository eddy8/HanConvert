Questions about the character 孙

How is 孙 pronounced in Mandarin?

孙 is pronounced sūn in Mandarin. It means grandchild, descendant, or the Chinese surname Sun.

What are the radical and stroke count for 孙?

孙 uses the 子 radical and has 6 strokes in the form shown on this page.

What are the Simplified and Traditional forms of 孙?

The common Simplified form is 孙; the Traditional form is 孫.
