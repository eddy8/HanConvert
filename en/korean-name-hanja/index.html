How to search Korean name Hanja

  1. Enter one Hangul reading, one Hanja or a Korean gloss.
  2. Compare the glyph, designated readings and descriptions.
  3. Confirm the final choice again on the Korean court service before registration.

FAQ

Can I rely on this result for official name registration?

Do not rely on it alone. The data comes from the public court lookup, but policies and glyph support can change; confirm on the official system before filing.

Why are fewer glyphs displayed than all court codes?

Some extension forms use court-specific glyph encoding that ordinary browsers cannot render reliably, so this page lists standard Unicode forms only.

Can one Hanja be used with different name readings?

Some can. The page lists designated readings returned by the court lookup, subject to the current registration rules.
