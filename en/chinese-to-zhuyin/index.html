How do you convert Chinese to Zhuyin?

Paste Chinese text above. The tool picks each character's Mandarin reading and writes it in Zhuyin (Bopomofo), the 37-symbol phonetic system taught in Taiwan. Tone 1 has no mark, tones 2-4 use ˊ ˇ ˋ, and the neutral tone puts ˙ in front. For example, 注音 becomes ㄓㄨˋ ㄧㄣ (zhù yīn).

Taiwan readings

Chinese to Zhuyin FAQ

What is the difference between Zhuyin and Pinyin?

Both write Mandarin pronunciation. Pinyin uses Latin letters and is standard in Mainland China. Zhuyin, also called Bopomofo, uses 37 symbols such as ㄅㄆㄇㄈ and is used in Taiwan. 注音 is zhù yīn in Pinyin and ㄓㄨˋ ㄧㄣ in Zhuyin.

Does the converter handle polyphonic characters?

Yes. Readings follow word context, so 銀行 (bank) gets ㄏㄤˊ and 行走 (walk) gets ㄒㄧㄥˊ. If a reading is still wrong, click that character in the preview, choose another reading, and the text result updates too.

Why does Taiwan Zhuyin differ from Mainland Pinyin for some words?

Taiwan's Ministry of Education standard reads some characters differently. 星期 is ㄒㄧㄥ ㄑㄧˊ (Mainland xīngqī), 垃圾 is ㄌㄜˋ ㄙㄜˋ (Mainland lājī), and 危險 is ㄨㄟˊ ㄒㄧㄢˇ. Switch to Mainland Mandarin if you need Putonghua readings.

Can I convert Pinyin to Zhuyin?

Yes. Enter Pinyin without Chinese characters, such as zhù yīn or zhu4 yin1, and it converts directly to ㄓㄨˋ ㄧㄣ. Both tone marks and tone numbers work.

Should 一 and 不 show their tone changes?

By default they keep their dictionary tones, so 一個 is ㄧ ㄍㄜˋ and 不對 is ㄅㄨˋ ㄉㄨㄟˋ. Turn on the tone-change option to show the spoken tones ㄧˊ ㄍㄜˋ and ㄅㄨˊ ㄉㄨㄟˋ.

How do I put Zhuyin into Word or a worksheet?

Copy the Hanzi(Zhuyin) text, print the side-by-side layout, or download a white PNG image. The image keeps any readings you corrected in the preview.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Readings and Zhuyin are produced in your browser. The page only loads the converter code from a public CDN and never sends the text you enter.
