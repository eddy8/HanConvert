Japanese Kanji dictionary questions

How can I find a Kanji when I do not know its reading?

Choose its radical and approximate total strokes, or type a component you can recognize.

What is the difference between On-yomi and Kun-yomi?

On readings generally derive from historical Chinese readings; Kun readings map Kanji to native Japanese words. Vocabulary context still determines the actual reading.

How often is the dictionary updated?

The local index is rebuilt from current EDRDG KANJIDIC2 and KRADFILE data and checked at least monthly as required by the licence.
