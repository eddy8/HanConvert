FAQ

How do I find the reading and meaning of a Korean Hanja?

Type or paste the character to see its Korean reading and meaning (hun). If you cannot read or type it, draw it first with Korean Hanja handwriting, then open the result here.

Can I search Hanja by Hangul reading?

Yes. Enter a reading such as 학 to list every Hanja with that sound, including 學, 鶴, 虐, 謔, and 壑, then narrow the list by radical and total strokes.

How many Hanja does the dictionary include?

It covers 27,581 Hanja with Korean readings and marks the 9,495 name Hanja published by the Korean courts, 9,118 of which display correctly in browsers.

What does 옥편 mean?

옥편 originally meant a radical-indexed Chinese character dictionary and now commonly describes online Hanja lookup by radical, strokes or reading.

Can I show only Hanja allowed in Korean names?

Yes, but name-registration rules can change. Confirm the character again on the Korean court's official service before filing documents.

Why do some rare Hanja lack radical or stroke data?

The Korean reading dictionary covers more extension characters than the site's glyph dataset, so a few records contain readings and glosses only.
