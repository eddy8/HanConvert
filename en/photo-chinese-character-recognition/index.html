Chinese character recognition · Image to text · Pinyin

Extract Chinese text from images, then inspect each Hanzi

Chinese OCR turns the Chinese characters in a photo, screenshot or scan into text you can copy. Take, upload or paste an image here, start recognition and you usually get the result within seconds, with Pinyin above each character when you want it. Use this free Chinese OCR online as an image-to-text tool for books, menus, signs, worksheets, chat screenshots and printed documents in Simplified or Traditional Chinese.

Take, upload or paste

Open the phone camera or photo library. On a computer, choose a file, drag an image onto the preview, or paste a fresh screenshot with Ctrl+V (⌘V on a Mac).

Pinyin above every character

Turn on Show Pinyin to see tone-marked Pinyin over each Hanzi, chosen from the surrounding word: 重 reads zhòng in 重要 and chóng in 重庆. Copy all of the Pinyin in one click.

Tap a Hanzi to look it up

Select any character in the result to see its Pinyin, stroke count and Unicode code point, then open the stroke-order animation or component lookup.

How to recognize Chinese characters from a photo

  1. Photograph the Chinese text or choose an image. On a computer you can also drag in an image or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.
  2. Check the automatically cropped preview, select “Recognize Chinese characters” and wait a few seconds.
  3. Copy the text or its Pinyin, listen to it read aloud, or tap a Hanzi to check its stroke order and structure.

Chinese photo OCR questions

Can it recognize more than one Chinese character?

Yes. An image may contain one Hanzi, a phrase or several lines. Copy the complete result or tap any recognized character to look it up.

Does it support Simplified and Traditional Chinese?

It supports common Simplified and Traditional characters. Blur, stylized lettering, glare, steep angles and busy backgrounds can reduce OCR accuracy.

Does recognition run entirely in my browser?

No. Cropping and compression run in your browser, then the processed image is uploaded to the JianFan.app OCR service for recognition.

How is photo OCR different from handwriting recognition?

This page reads Chinese text from a photo or screenshot. The handwriting tool matches one character from strokes drawn in its writing grid.

Can this Chinese OCR convert an image to editable text?

Yes. Upload a photo, screenshot or scan containing Simplified or Traditional Chinese. The Chinese image-to-text result can be copied in full, or you can tap a Hanzi to look it up.

Can I paste a screenshot instead of saving a file?

Yes. Take a screenshot with Win+Shift+S on Windows or Control+Shift+Command+4 on a Mac, return to this page and press Ctrl+V (⌘V). You can also drag an image file onto the preview.

Can it show Pinyin for Chinese text in a picture?

Yes. After recognition, select Show Pinyin to add tone-marked Pinyin above each character, or Copy Pinyin to copy it as text. Polyphonic characters follow the word, for example 重要 zhòng yào and 重庆 chóng qìng, but check names and rare words.

How can I read Chinese from a blurry photo or an old document?

Fill the frame with the text, shoot straight on in even light, and take a screenshot of on-screen text instead of photographing the screen. For one unclear character, crop it large and recognize it alone, or draw it in the handwriting tool.

Can I convert recognized Traditional Chinese to Simplified?

Yes. Copy the result and paste it into the Traditional to Simplified converter, which converts by word, for example 頭髮 to 头发 and 軟體 to 软件.

What image formats and sizes are supported?

PNG, JPEG, WebP, HEIC and HEIF up to 24 MB. The browser trims empty borders and, when needed, scales the longer side to 1,600 pixels and compresses the image below 2 MB before upload.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

It is free and needs no sign-up. To prevent abuse, sending many requests in a short time shows a message asking you to try again later.

Can it read the recognized Chinese aloud?

Yes. Select Read aloud to hear the full text in a Mandarin voice from your browser or system, and select it again to stop. After choosing a character, Hear this character plays it alone. Voices depend on your device; on-device voices are preferred, and if only an online voice exists, the text is sent to the browser vendor's speech service.

What does OCR mean?

OCR stands for optical character recognition: software reads the text in an image and turns it into editable text. This page is an online OCR for Chinese characters, so you can copy the recognized Simplified or Traditional text, add Pinyin and look up each Hanzi.
