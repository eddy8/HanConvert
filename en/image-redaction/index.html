Manual image censoring, local AI review and safer export

Common image redaction jobs

Screenshots and support conversations

Hide usernames, avatars, order numbers, customer records, email addresses and access keys before sharing a screen capture.

IDs and document photos

Cover document numbers, addresses, signatures and payment details. Use a solid box for formal or high-risk information.

Why every AI suggestion needs a human review

Glare, low resolution, unusual type, angled photos and busy backgrounds all reduce detection accuracy. The scan is a head start, not a guarantee against missed data.

Zoom around the full image before exporting, including corners, reflections, notification bars and background signs. Give solid boxes extra margin around truly confidential text.

How to redact an image online

  1. Choose, drop or paste the image you want to protect.
  2. Drag boxes or paint with the brush over what to hide, or run the AI scan and review each suggestion.
  3. Choose black box, pixelation or blur, then adjust region size and strength.
  4. Compare with the original, then download the new image or choose Copy image to paste it into a chat.

Image redaction questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Decoding, manual edits, face detection, OCR and export run in this browser. An internet connection is used only to download the AI models on first use.

Can a pixelated or blurred image be reversed?

A solid box replaces the original pixels, so it cannot be undone. Blur and pixelation over text can sometimes be guessed or reconstructed, especially with small blocks over large text, so cover ID numbers, card numbers and passwords with a solid box and a little margin. The export is a newly rendered single-layer image without the original pixels or editable layers.

Can I paint over an area with a brush?

Yes. Switch the tool to Brush, set its size, and drag to paint over hair, signatures or other irregular shapes. Solid fill, pixelation and blur all work with the brush; undo a stroke, or select it to move or delete it.

What sensitive data can the AI scan find?

It suggests faces and likely phone numbers, emails, IDs, payment cards, URLs, IP addresses, plates and text lines labeled as names or addresses. Review every result.

Does export remove photo location data?

The tool recreates a JPG, PNG or WebP with Canvas, so it normally does not copy EXIF fields such as GPS, device model or capture time from the source file.

How do I redact a screenshot?

Take the screenshot, press Ctrl / Cmd + V on this page, draw boxes or run the AI scan to find phone numbers and avatars, then choose Copy image and paste the result straight into a chat or email.

Can I blur faces on my phone?

Yes. Open the page in your phone browser, pick a photo, drag over each face with your finger, and download the result. No app is needed.

Is it free, and does it add a watermark?

It is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no usage limit.

Which image formats and sizes work?

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP and other images your browser can open, up to 30 MB. Images above 24 megapixels or 6,000 pixels on the long edge are scaled down for stable processing. Export as PNG, JPG or WebP.
