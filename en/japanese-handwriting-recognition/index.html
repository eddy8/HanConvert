Kanji handwriting recognition questions

Must I use the correct stroke order?

Not exactly, although closer stroke direction, shape, and placement generally produce better candidates.

Why does the first recognition take longer?

The first drawing triggers a verified download of about 6.7 MB of Japanese reference patterns. Browser caching helps later visits.

Is my handwriting uploaded?

No. Strokes and matching remain in this browser. Only the pinned public pattern file is downloaded.
